Chromatic teaches color theory by doing — then hands you the tools.

THE LOOP
Learn it: seven structured lessons, from the color wheel to WCAG contrast.
Build it: a harmony wheel with HSV and perceptual OKLCH spacing, tonal palettes,
style moods, photo extraction, gradient blending in three color spaces.
Wear it: Theme Studio turns one seed color into a complete, contrast-verified
Material 3 scheme — and can re-theme this very app with it.

TOOLS INCLUDED
• Harmony explorer — six classic schemes, OKLCH hue spacing, color-blind-safe preview
• Contrast checker — WCAG 2.1 ratios, APCA, live preview, one-tap auto-fix to AA
• Contrast grid — all 100 tone pairings, measured and graded
• Palette builder — tonal scales, photo extraction, six style moods, export to
  JSON / CSS / Compose / Android XML / PNG
• Vision simulator — precise Machado CVD model on colors, live whole-app preview
• Mix lab & Illusion lab — additive vs subtractive physics, simultaneous contrast,
  driven by your own hands
• Four quiz games — knowledge, hue matching, naming, lightness discrimination

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

online. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Crash reports stay on your device
unless you choose to share them.
